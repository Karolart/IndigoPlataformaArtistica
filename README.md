# 🎨 Indigo Plataforma Artística  
*A digital space that connects artists, creators, and cultural entrepreneurs from Medellín and beyond.*

👉 **Live Site:** https://indigo-plataforma-artistica.vercel.app/  
👉 **Repository:** https://github.com/Karolart/IndigoPlataformaArtistica

---

## 🌟 Overview

**Indigo Plataforma Artística** is a web platform designed to showcase, connect, and empower artists from **Medellín, the Aburrá Valley, and any creative individual who wants to join**.  
The project aims to become a digital ecosystem where artists can:

- Present their artistic services
- Share portfolios, social links, and their creative journey  
- Be discovered by clients, event organizers, and collaborators  
- Receive direct contact from people interested in their work  

The vision behind Indigo is to create a space where **art becomes visible, accessible, and valued**—a meeting point for community, culture, and entrepreneurship.

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Vision

Our mission is to build a central hub that highlights creative talent of all kinds, such as:

- Visual artists  
- Photographers  
- Dancers  
- Musicians  
- Tattoo artists  
- Graphic designers  
- Artisans  
- Creators of all disciplines  

Indigo provides visibility for independent artists and allows anyone to explore their work in a clean, elegant interface.

**This project seeks to support the local artistic community by giving them a digital home where they can grow.**

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Vite + React  
- **Styling:** CSS Modules  
- **Data handling:** JSON-based artist catalog  
- **Deployment:** Vercel  
- **Future goals:**  
  - Express + MongoDB backend  
  - Editable artist profiles  
  - Authentication  
  - Permanent QR access  
  - PDF viewer for portfolios  

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎭 **Artist Directory:** A visually pleasing layout where each artist has their own card.  
- 🖼️ **Portfolio Viewer:** Elegant PDF viewer to display work or menus/services.  
- 📱 **Easy Contact Access:** WhatsApp, Instagram, and other links directly available.  
- 🎨 **Clean & modern UI** inspired by creative markets and artistic branding.  
- 👥 **Expandable:** Supports up to 100+ artist profiles using dynamic data files.
